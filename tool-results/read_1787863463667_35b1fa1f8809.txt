     1→# 📈 Budget King BD — Analytics & Reporting
     2→
     3→For Budget King BD, analytics should be designed as **operational analytics first**, not a giant BI system. Admin should quickly understand **what is selling, what is happening with orders, who the customers are, and where problems exist**.
     4→
     5→Core structure:
     6→
     7→```text
     8→Orders
     9→ ├── Sales
    10→ ├── Customers
    11→ ├── Products
    12→ ├── Rewards
    13→ ├── Groups
    14→ └── Agents
    15→        ↓
    16→   Analytics Layer
    17→        ↓
    18→ Admin Dashboard / Reports
    19→```
    20→
    21→---
    22→

   545→# 18. 🧑‍💼 Agent performance
   546→
   547→Agent analytics should focus on **work quality and efficiency**, not just how many orders they touch.
   548→
   549→Track:
   550→
   551→* Orders assigned
   552→* Orders confirmed
   553→* Orders cancelled
   554→* Confirmation time
   555→* Processing time
   556→* Shipping time
   557→* Delivery success
   558→* Failed deliveries
   559→* Customer contact attempts
   560→* Return/exchange handling
   561→
   562→Example:
   563→
   564→```text
   565→Agent Performance
   566→
   567→Agent       Orders   Confirmed   Failed   Avg Confirm
   568→------------------------------------------------------
   569→Rahim         184       171         13       18 min
   570→Karim         162       151         11       24 min
   571→Hasan         143       136          7       21 min
   572→```
   573→
   574→---
   575→
   576→# 19. Agent performance shouldn't encourage bad behavior
   577→
   578→Don't rank agents simply by:
   579→
   580→> "Who confirmed the most orders?"
   581→
   582→An agent could confirm everything without properly verifying COD orders.
   583→
   584→Better metrics:
   585→
   586→```text
   587→Confirmation rate
   588→Delivery success
   589→Cancellation accuracy
   590→Average processing time
   591→Customer complaints
   592→```
   593→
   594→Eventually you can create a weighted performance score.

   892→# 30. Analytics data architecture
   893→
   894→Don't make the frontend calculate everything from thousands of orders.
   895→
   896→Instead:
   897→
   898→```text
   899→Orders
   900→Products
   901→Customers
   902→Rewards
   903→Groups
   904→Agents
   905→       ↓
   906→Database queries / views
   907→       ↓
   908→Analytics layer
   909→       ↓
   910→Dashboard
   911→```
   912→
   913→For frequently used metrics, Supabase/PostgreSQL views or materialized aggregates can be used later.

   917→# 31. Don't store every metric unnecessarily
   918→
   919→For example, don't blindly store:
   920→
   921→```text
   922→users.total_orders
   923→users.total_spent
   924→users.average_order_value
   925→```
   926→
   927→as manually editable values.
   928→
   929→These can become inconsistent.
   930→
   931→Prefer:
   932→
   933→```text
   934→orders
   935→   ↓
   936→SQL aggregation
   937→   ↓
   938→Analytics
   939→```
   940→
   941→For high-traffic metrics, cache/precompute them later.

  1037→# 35. Final analytics architecture
  1038→
  1039→```text
  1040→                         DATABASE
  1041→                            │
  1042→       ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
  1043→       ↓                    ↓                    ↓
  1044→     Orders              Products            Customers
  1045→       │                    │                    │
  1046→       ├──────────┐         │                    │
  1047→       ↓          ↓         ↓                    ↓
  1048→    Delivery    Rewards   Inventory          Groups
  1049→       │          │         │                    │
  1050→       └──────────┴─────────┴────────────────────┘
  1051→                            │
  1052→                            ↓
  1053→                    ANALYTICS LAYER
  1054→                            │
  1055→        ┌───────────────────┼───────────────────┐
  1056→        ↓                   ↓                   ↓
  1057→      Admin              Agent              Reports
  1058→    Dashboard           Dashboard           / Export
  1059→```
  1060→
  1061→### The key principle
  1062→
  1063→**Your analytics should be a read-only interpretation of your transactional systems.** Don't make analytics itself responsible for business logic.
  1064→
  1065→That gives you a clean separation:
  1066→
  1067→```text
  1068→Order system    → records what happened
  1069→Inventory       → records stock
  1070→Rewards         → records coins/vouchers
  1071→Groups          → records group activity
  1072→Analytics       → explains what happened
  1073→```
  1074→
  1075→That architecture will scale much better as Budget King BD grows beyond shirts.
  1076→# ⚙️ Budget King BD — Business Rules
  1077→
  1078→This section defines **what the system is allowed to do and under what conditions**. These rules should be agreed upon before implementation because they will affect the database, backend logic, RLS, order workflow, rewards, and UI.

  1082→## 1. 💵 COD Rules
  1083→
  1084→Since Budget King BD accepts **Cash on Delivery only**:
  1085→
  1086→### Basic rules
  1087→
  1088→* All customer orders use COD.
  1089→* No online payment status is required.
  1090→* Order is not considered financially paid until delivery/payment is confirmed.
  1091→* Customer sees the total payable amount before confirming.
  1092→* Delivery charge is included in the final COD amount.
  1093→* Redeemed vouchers reduce the COD amount.
  1094→* Coins themselves cannot be used directly as payment unless converted into a voucher.
  1095→
  1096→### COD order flow
  1097→
  1098→```text
  1099→Cart
  1100→ ↓
  1101→Checkout
  1102→ ↓
  1103→COD selected automatically
  1104→ ↓
  1105→Order placed
  1106→ ↓
  1107→Agent confirms
  1108→ ↓
  1109→Order prepared
  1110→ ↓
  1111→Shipped
  1112→ ↓
  1113→Delivered
  1114→ ↓
  1115→COD collected
  1116→ ↓
  1117→Order completed
  1118→```
  1119→
  1120→### Important
  1121→
  1122→Don't let the customer enter an arbitrary COD amount.
  1123→
  1124→```text
  1125→Product subtotal
  1126→+ Delivery
  1127→- Voucher
  1128→= Final COD amount
  1129→```
  1130→
  1131→The server calculates this.
  1132→

  1135→# 2. ❌ Cancellation Rules
  1136→
  1137→You need to distinguish **customer cancellation** from **admin/agent cancellation**.
  1138→
  1139→### Customer cancellation
  1140→
  1141→Customer can cancel only before a certain stage.
  1142→
  1143→Recommended:
  1144→
  1145→```text
  1146→PENDING
  1147→   ↓
  1148→CONFIRMED
  1149→   ↓
  1150→PROCESSING
  1151→   ↓
  1152→SHIPPED
  1153→   ↓
  1154→DELIVERED
  1155→```
  1156→
  1157→Allow cancellation:
  1158→
  1159→```text
  1160→PENDING       ✅
  1161→CONFIRMED     ✅/configurable
  1162→PROCESSING    ❌
  1163→SHIPPED       ❌
  1164→DELIVERED     ❌
  1165→```
  1166→
  1167→Once shipped, the customer should use the return process instead.
  1168→
  1169→### Cancellation reasons
  1170→
  1171→Use predefined reasons:
  1172→
  1173→* Changed my mind
  1174→* Ordered by mistake
  1175→* Wrong product
  1176→* Wrong size/color
  1177→* Delivery taking too long
  1178→* Other
  1179→
  1180→For "Other":
  1181→
  1182→> Require a short explanation.
  1183→

  1185→
  1186→# 3. Agent cancellation
  1187→
  1188→Agents should **not be able to cancel arbitrarily**.
  1189→
  1190→For example:
  1191→
  1192→```text
  1193→Agent
  1194→ ↓
  1195→Select cancellation reason
  1196→ ↓
  1197→Confirm
  1198→ ↓
  1199→System records action
  1200→```
  1201→
  1202→Possible reasons:
  1203→
  1204→* Customer unreachable
  1205→* Customer requested cancellation
  1206→* Product unavailable
  1207→* Invalid address
  1208→* Duplicate order
  1209→* Other
  1210→
  1211→Every cancellation should be recorded in the order history/audit log.
  1212→

  1221→    ↓

  1228→

  1323→# 9. Return vs exchange
  1324→
  1325→Keep them separate.
  1326→
  1327→### Return
  1328→
  1329→```text

  1331→↓
  1332→Order/item refunded according to policy
  1333→```
  1334→
  1335→### Exchange
  1336→
  1337→```text

  1347→# 10. 💰 COD refund policy
  1348→

  1365→# 11. 🪙 Rewards during cancellation/return
  1366→
  1367→This must connect directly to your rewards ledger.
  1368→
  1369→Suppose:
  1370→
  1371→```text
  1372→Order = ৳2,000
  1373→↓
  1374→Customer earns 2,000 coins
  1375→```
  1376→
  1377→Then order is cancelled before completion.
  1378→
  1379→The system should reverse the earned coins:
  1380→
  1381→```text
  1382→+2,000 coins
  1383→        ↓
  1384→Order cancelled
  1385→        ↓
  1386→-2,000 coins
  1387→```
  1388→
  1389→**Never directly edit the user's coin balance.**
  1390→
  1391→Use ledger entries.
  1392→

  1395→# 12. Rewards after return
  1396→
  1397→Same principle.
  1398→
  1399→```text
  1400→Delivered
  1401→↓

  1403→↓
  1404→Return approved
  1405→↓
  1406→-2,000 coins
  1407→```
  1408→
  1409→If the customer has already spent those coins, you need a defined policy.

  1411→I'd recommend:
  1412→
  1413→> The system can allow the resulting coin balance to go negative internally, or mark the account as having a rewards debt.
  1414→
  1415→But I would **not** silently confiscate unrelated coins without an auditable transaction.

  1419→# 13. 🪙 Coin earning rule
  1420→
  1421→Based on your current concept:
  1422→
  1423→```text
  1424→Purchase amount ≥ ৳2,000
  1425→        ↓
  1426→Earn 2,000 coins
  1427→```
  1428→
  1429→But you should define exactly what "purchase amount" means.
  1430→
  1431→I recommend:
  1432→
  1433→> Coins are calculated from **eligible product subtotal after discounts, excluding delivery charges**.
  1434→
  1435→Example:
  1436→
  1437→```text
  1438→Products:       ৳2,200
  1439→Delivery:         ৳80
  1440→Voucher:          ৳100

  1442→Eligible amount: ৳2,100

  1444→
  1445→If the threshold is ৳2,000:
  1446→
  1447→```text

  1449→```

  1880→# 31. Price protection
  1881→
  1882→Very important.
  1883→
  1884→Suppose:
  1885→
  1886→```text

  1888→৳699
  1889→```

  1892→
  1893→```text

  1895→```
  1896→
  1897→The checkout should use the **current valid price**, not blindly trust the cart.
  1898→
  1899→At order creation, store:
  1900→
  1901→```text
