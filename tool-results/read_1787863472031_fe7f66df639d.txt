     1→     1→# 👥 Budget King BD — Group Shopping System
     2→     2→
     3→     3→The group-shopping system can become one of **Budget King BD's main differentiating features**. I would design it around this flow:
     4→     4→
     5→     5→```text
     6→     6→Create Group
     7→     7→     ↓
     8→     8→Share Group Code
     9→     9→     ↓
    10→    10→Members Join
    11→    11→     ↓
    12→    12→Share Products
    13→    13→     ↓
    14→    14→Vote / Discuss
    15→    15→     ↓
    16→    16→Add Products to Group Cart
    17→    17→     ↓
    18→    18→Group Checkout
    19→    19→     ↓
    20→    20→One Common Delivery
    21→    21→     ↓
    22→    22→Delivery Cost Shared
    23→    23→```
    24→    24→
    25→    25→Think of it like a **shared shopping room**: everyone can suggest shirts, everyone can vote, but only authorized people can finalize the group's purchase.
    26→    26→
    27→    27→---
    28→    28→

    61→    61→
    62→    62→Example:
    63→    63→
    64→    64→```text
    65→    65→BK7X29
    66→    66→```

    99→    99→# 3. 👤 Group roles
   100→   100→
   101→   101→Keep group-specific roles simple.
   102→   102→
   103→   103→### Group Owner
   104→   104→
   105→   105→The person who created the group.
   106→   106→
   107→   107→Can:
   108→   108→
   109→   109→* Change group settings
   110→   110→* Remove members
   111→   111→* Change member permissions
   112→   112→* Manage group cart
   113→   113→* Start group checkout
   114→   114→* Set delivery address
   115→   115→* Close/delete group
   116→   116→
   117→   117→### Group Member
   118→   118→
   119→   119→Can:
   120→   120→
   121→   121→* View group
   122→   122→* Share products
   123→   123→* Vote
   124→   124→* Comment/discuss
   125→   125→* Add products to group cart
   126→   126→* See group cart
   127→   127→* Remove **their own** items
   128→   128→
   129→   129→### Optional: Group Admin
   130→   130→
   131→   131→I'd initially **avoid this**.
   132→   132→
   133→   133→If a group becomes large, you can allow the owner to promote someone to:
   134→   134→
   135→   135→> Group Admin
   136→   136→
   137→   137→But this isn't necessary for V1.
   138→   138→
   139→   139→---
   140→   140→
   141→   141→# 4. 🔐 Group permissions
   142→   142→
   143→   143→I'd use something like:
   144→   144→
   145→   145→| Action                       | Owner | Member |
   146→   146→| ---------------------------- | ----: | -----: |
   147→   147→| View group                   |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   148→   148→| Join                         |     — |      — |
   149→   149→| Invite                       |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   150→   150→| Share product                |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   151→   151→| Vote                         |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   152→   152→| Comment                      |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   153→   153→| Add to group cart            |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   154→   154→| Remove own cart item         |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   155→   155→| Remove another member's item |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   156→   156→| Edit group settings          |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   157→   157→| Remove member                |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   158→   158→| Start checkout               |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   159→   159→| Set common address           |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   160→   160→| Close group                  |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   161→   161→
   162→   162→The key principle:
   163→   163→
   164→   164→> **Members control their own shopping; the owner controls the group.**
   165→   165→

   270→   270→# 8. One vote per member
   271→   271→
   272→   272→A member should only have **one active vote per shared product**.
   273→   273→
   274→   274→Example:
   275→   275→
   276→   276→```text

   278→   278→Karim → 👍

   280→   280→```
   281→   281→
   282→   282→Results:
   283→   283→
   284→   284→```text
   285→   285→👍 2
   286→   286→👎 1
   287→   287→```

   289→   289→If Rahim changes his mind:
   290→   290→
   291→   291→```text
   292→   292→👍 → 👎
   293→   293→```
   294→   294→
   295→   295→The system updates his vote rather than creating another vote.

   299→   299→# 9. Voting doesn't automatically add to cart
   300→   300→

   302→   302→
   303→   303→Someone votes:
   304→   304→
   305→   305→> 👍
   306→   306→
   307→   307→That should **not mean they want to buy it**.
   308→   308→
   309→   309→Instead:
   310→   310→
   311→   311→```text
   312→   312→Vote
   313→   313→  ≠
   314→   314→Purchase
   315→   315→```
   316→   316→
   317→   317→The user must explicitly select:
   318→   318→
   319→   319→> **Add to Group Cart**

   545→   545→# 17. How should delivery be split?
   546→   546→
   547→   547→I recommend **proportional splitting based on item subtotal**.
   548→   548→
   549→   549→Example:
   550→   550→
   551→   551→```text
   552→   552→Total product value = ৳2,896
   553→   553→Delivery = ৳80
   554→   554→```
   555→   555→
   556→   556→Rahim:
   557→   557→
   558→   558→```text
   559→   559→1398 / 2896 × 80
   560→   560→≈ ৳38.64
   561→   561→```
   562→   562→
   563→   563→Karim:
   564→   564→
   565→   565→```text
   566→   566→699 / 2896 × 80
   567→   567→≈ ৳19.31
   568→   568→```
   569→   569→
   570→   570→Hasan:
   571→   571→
   572→   572→```text
   573→   573→799 / 2896 × 80
   574→   574→≈ ৳22.05
   575→   575→```
   576→   576→
   577→   577→Total:
   578→   578→
   579→   579→```text
   580→   580→38.64 + 19.31 + 22.05
   581→   581→= ৳80
   582→   582→```
   583→   583→
   584→   584→This is fairer than simply splitting:
   585→   585→
   586→   586→```text
   587→   587→৳80 / 3 = ৳26.67
   588→   588→```
   589→   589→
   590→   590→because members may buy very different amounts.
   591→   591→

   593→   593→
   594→   594→# 18. But for COD...
   595→   595→
   596→   596→Here's the important operational problem.
   597→   597→
   598→   598→Suppose:
   599→   599→
   600→   600→```text
   601→   601→Rahim → ৳1,436.64
   602→   602→Karim → ৳718.31
   603→   603→Hasan → ৳821.05
   604→   604→```
   605→   605→
   606→   606→Are you going to ask the delivery agent to collect three separate payments?
   607→   607→
   608→   608→**I wouldn't.**
   609→   609→
   610→   610→For V1:
   611→   611→
   612→   612→### One group order = one COD payment
   613→   613→
   614→   614→The group owner chooses:
   615→   615→
   616→   616→> **COD payer**
   617→   617→
   618→   618→Example:
   619→   619→
   620→   620→```text
   621→   621→COD Payer:
   622→   622→Rahim
   623→   623→
   624→   624→Total:
   625→   625→৳2,976
   626→   626→```
   627→   627→
   628→   628→Rahim collects money from the others himself.
   629→   629→
   630→   630→The system can still show:
   631→   631→
   632→   632→```text
   633→   633→Rahim owes:    ৳1,436.64
   634→   634→Karim owes:      ৳718.31
   635→   635→Hasan owes:      ৳821.05
   636→   636→```
   637→   637→
   638→   638→But Budget King BD only needs to collect:
   639→   639→
   640→   640→```text
   641→   641→৳2,976
   642→   642→```
   643→   643→
   644→   644→from the delivery recipient.
   645→   645→
   646→   646→---
   647→   647→

  1201→  1201→# 📊 Budget King BD — Inventory System
  1202→  1202→
  1203→  1203→For Budget King BD, I recommend a **variant-based inventory system with reservations and an immutable movement history**.
  1204→  1204→
  1205→  1205→The core idea:
  1206→  1206→
  1207→  1207→```text
  1208→  1208→Product
  1209→  1209→   ↓
  1210→  1210→Variant
  1211→  1211→   ↓
  1212→  1212→Available Stock
  1213→  1213→   ↓
  1214→  1214→Reservation
  1215→  1215→   ↓
  1216→  1216→Order
  1217→  1217→   ↓
  1218→  1218→Delivered / Cancelled / Returned
  1219→  1219→```
  1220→  1220→
  1221→  1221→The key rule is: **customers never directly modify stock.** Every stock change happens through controlled server-side operations.
  1222→  1222→
  1223→  1223→---
  1224→  1224→
  1225→  1225→# 1. 👕 Stock belongs to variants
  1226→  1226→
  1227→  1227→Inventory should be attached to the **product variant**, not the product itself.
  1228→  1228→
  1229→  1229→Example:
  1230→  1230→
  1231→  1231→```text
  1232→  1232→Oxford Shirt
  1233→  1233→│
  1234→  1234→├── Black / M   → SKU: OS-BLK-M → 12
  1235→  1235→├── Black / L   → SKU: OS-BLK-L → 8
  1236→  1236→├── Black / XL  → SKU: OS-BLK-XL → 5
  1237→  1237→├── White / M   → SKU: OS-WHT-M → 10
  1238→  1238→└── White / L   → SKU: OS-WHT-L → 7
  1239→  1239→```
  1240→  1240→
  1241→  1241→Each variant has its own stock.
  1242→  1242→
  1243→  1243→This becomes essential when you eventually sell trousers, shoes, etc.
  1244→  1244→
  1245→  1245→**Analogy:** A clothing store doesn't have "20 Oxford shirts" as one pile—the warehouse has 20 specific combinations of size/color.
  1246→  1246→
  1247→  1247→---
  1248→  1248→
  1249→  1249→# 2. 🗄️ Inventory table
  1250→  1250→
  1251→  1251→Use:
  1252→  1252→
  1253→  1253→```text
  1254→  1254→inventory
  1255→  1255→├── variant_id
  1256→  1256→├── quantity
  1257→  1257→├── reserved_quantity
  1258→  1258→├── low_stock_threshold
  1259→  1259→├── updated_at
  1260→  1260→└── updated_by
  1261→  1261→```
  1262→  1262→
  1263→  1263→For example:
  1264→  1264→
  1265→  1265→```text
  1266→  1266→Variant: Black / XL
  1267→  1267→
  1268→  1268→quantity          = 10
  1269→  1269→reserved_quantity = 3
  1270→  1270→```
  1271→  1271→
  1272→  1272→Therefore:
  1273→  1273→
  1274→  1274→```text
  1275→  1275→Available stock = 10 - 3 = 7
  1276→  1276→```
  1277→  1277→
  1278→  1278→---
  1279→  1279→
  1280→  1280→# 3. 🟢 Stock states
  1281→  1281→
  1282→  1282→You essentially have:
  1283→  1283→
  1284→  1284→```text
  1285→  1285→TOTAL STOCK
  1286→  1286→     │
  1287→  1287→     ├── AVAILABLE
  1288→  1288→     │
  1289→  1289→     └── RESERVED
  1290→  1290→```
  1291→  1291→
  1292→  1292→Example:
  1293→  1293→
  1294→  1294→```text
  1295→  1295→Total:      20
  1296→  1296→Reserved:    4
  1297→  1297→Available:  16
  1298→  1298→```
  1299→  1299→
  1300→  1300→Don't create a separate manually editable `available_quantity`.
  1301→  1301→
  1302→  1302→Calculate it:
  1303→  1303→
  1304→  1304→```text
  1305→  1305→available = quantity - reserved_quantity
  1306→  1306→```
  1307→  1307→
  1308→  1308→That prevents inconsistencies.
  1309→  1309→
  1310→  1310→---
  1311→  1311→
  1312→  1312→# 4. 🛒 Stock reservation
  1313→  1313→
  1314→  1314→This is particularly important for COD.
  1315→  1315→
  1316→  1316→Suppose:
  1317→  1317→
  1318→  1318→```text
  1319→  1319→Stock = 10
  1320→  1320→```
  1321→  1321→
  1322→  1322→Customer A orders:
  1323→  1323→
  1324→  1324→```text
  1325→  1325→5 shirts
  1326→  1326→```
  1327→  1327→
  1328→  1328→While the order is pending:
  1329→  1329→
  1330→  1330→```text
  1331→  1331→quantity          = 10
  1332→  1332→reserved_quantity = 5
  1333→  1333→available         = 5
  1334→  1334→```
  1335→  1335→
  1336→  1336→Now Customer B can't successfully order 6.
  1337→  1337→
  1338→  1338→The server sees:
  1339→  1339→
  1340→  1340→```text
  1341→  1341→Available = 5
  1342→  1342→Requested = 6
  1343→  1343→
  1344→  1344→❌ Insufficient stock
  1345→  1345→```
  1346→  1346→
  1347→  1347→---
  1348→  1348→
  1349→  1349→# 5. Why reservation is necessary
  1350→  1350→
  1351→  1351→Without reservations:
  1352→  1352→
  1353→  1353→```text
  1354→  1354→Stock = 5
  1355→  1355→
  1356→  1356→Customer A → orders 5
  1357→  1357→Customer B → orders 5
  1358→  1358→```
  1359→  1359→
  1360→  1360→Both requests might see:
  1361→  1361→
  1362→  1362→```text
  1363→  1363→Stock = 5
  1364→  1364→```
  1365→  1365→
  1366→  1366→You end up with:
  1367→  1367→
  1368→  1368→```text
  1369→  1369→10 orders
  1370→  1370→5 products
  1371→  1371→```
  1372→  1372→
  1373→  1373→That's an **overselling/race-condition problem**.
  1374→  1374→
  1375→  1375→Reservation acts like putting a product into a physical shopping basket and marking it "held."
  1376→  1376→
  1377→  1377→---
  1378→  1378→
  1379→  1379→# 6. When should stock be reserved?
  1380→  1380→
  1381→  1381→I'd recommend:
  1382→  1382→
  1383→  1383→```text
  1384→  1384→Order successfully created
  1385→  1385→        ↓
  1386→  1386→Stock reservation
  1387→  1387→```
  1388→  1388→
  1389→  1389→So:
  1390→  1390→
  1391→  1391→```text
  1392→  1392→PENDING
  1393→  1393→   ↓
  1394→  1394→Reserved
  1395→  1395→```
  1396→  1396→
  1397→  1397→Then the reservation remains while the order is being processed.
  1398→  1398→
  1399→  1399→---
  1400→  1400→
  1401→  1401→# 7. Reservation lifecycle
  1402→  1402→
  1403→  1403→```text
  1404→  1404→Order Created
  1405→  1405→      ↓
  1406→  1406→Reserve Stock
  1407→  1407→      ↓
  1408→  1408→PENDING
  1409→  1409→      │
  1410→  1410→      ├── Cancelled → Release Reservation
  1411→  1411→      │
  1412→  1412→      ↓
  1413→  1413→CONFIRMED
  1414→  1414→      ↓
  1415→  1415→PROCESSING
  1416→  1416→      ↓
  1417→  1417→SHIPPED
  1418→  1418→      ↓
  1419→  1419→DELIVERED
  1420→  1420→      ↓
  1421→  1421→Convert Reservation → Sold
  1422→  1422→```
  1423→  1423→
  1424→  1424→The important distinction:
  1425→  1425→
  1426→  1426→### Reserved ≠ Sold
  1427→  1427→
  1428→  1428→A pending order hasn't actually resulted in a completed sale yet.
  1429→  1429→
  1430→  1430→---
  1431→  1431→
  1432→  1432→# 8. 📦 When order is shipped
  1433→  1433→
  1434→  1434→At shipping:
  1435→  1435→
  1436→  1436→```text
  1437→  1437→Reserved
  1438→  1438→    ↓
  1439→  1439→Sold
  1440→  1440→```
  1441→  1441→
  1442→  1442→Conceptually:
  1443→  1443→
  1444→  1444→```text
  1445→  1445→quantity = quantity - sold_quantity
  1446→  1446→reserved_quantity = reserved_quantity - reserved_quantity
  1447→  1447→```
  1448→  1448→
  1449→  1449→Example:
  1450→  1450→
  1451→  1451→Before:

  1459→  1459→After shipping 3:
  1460→  1460→
  1461→  1461→```text
  1462→  1462→Total stock: 7
  1463→  1463→Reserved:    0
  1464→  1464→Available:   7

  1469→  1469→# 9. ❌ Cancellation
  1470→  1470→
  1471→  1471→Suppose:
  1472→  1472→
  1473→  1473→```text
  1474→  1474→Stock = 10
  1475→  1475→Reserved = 3
  1476→  1476→Available = 7
  1477→  1477→```
  1478→  1478→

  1484→  1484→

  1487→  1487→3 → 0

  1491→  1491→

  1502→  1502→# 10. 🚚 Delivery failure
  1503→  1503→
  1504→  1504→If the order was shipped but couldn't be delivered:
  1505→  1505→
  1506→  1506→```text
  1507→  1507→SHIPPED
  1508→  1508→   ↓
  1509→  1509→DELIVERY_FAILED
  1510→  1510→```
  1511→  1511→
  1512→  1512→You need to distinguish between:
  1513→  1513→
  1514→  1514→### Reattempt
  1515→  1515→
  1516→  1516→```text
  1517→  1517→DELIVERY_FAILED
  1518→  1518→      ↓
  1519→  1519→SHIPPED
  1520→  1520→```
  1521→  1521→
  1522→  1522→No inventory change.
  1523→  1523→
  1524→  1524→### Order permanently cancelled
  1525→  1525→
  1526→  1526→Then the item comes back into your inventory:
  1527→  1527→
  1528→  1528→```text
  1529→  1529→Cancelled
  1530→  1530→   ↓
  1531→  1531→Returned to warehouse
  1532→  1532→   ↓
  1533→  1533→Stock + quantity
  1534→  1534→```
  1535→  1535→
  1536→  1536→But this should happen when the physical goods are actually back—not merely when the order is marked cancelled.
  1537→  1537→

  1901→  1901→# 22. Atomic stock reservation
  1902→  1902→
  1903→  1903→This is technically important.
  1904→  1904→
  1905→  1905→Two customers might simultaneously try to buy the last shirt:
  1906→  1906→
  1907→  1907→```text
  1908→  1908→Stock = 1
  1909→  1909→
  1910→  1910→Customer A → 1
  1911→  1911→Customer B → 1
  1912→  1912→```
  1913→  1913→
  1914→  1914→The database operation must be atomic so that **only one succeeds**.
  1915→  1915→
  1916→  1916→Conceptually:
  1917→  1917→
  1918→  1918→```text
  1919→  1919→BEGIN TRANSACTION
  1920→  1920→
  1921→  1921→Check available stock
  1922→  1922→        ↓
  1923→  1923→If available >= requested
  1924→  1924→        ↓
  1925→  1925→Reserve stock
  1926→  1926→        ↓
  1927→  1927→Create order
  1928→  1928→
  1929→  1929→COMMIT
  1930→  1930→```
  1931→  1931→
  1932→  1932→Otherwise:
  1933→  1933→
  1934→  1934→```text
  1935→  1935→Customer A → SUCCESS
  1936→  1936→Customer B → SUCCESS
  1937→  1937→Stock → -1 ❌
  1938→  1938→```
