     1→# 👥 Budget King BD — Group Shopping System
     2→
     3→The group-shopping system can become one of **Budget King BD's main differentiating features**. I would design it around this flow:
     4→
     5→```text
     6→Create Group
     7→     ↓
     8→Share Group Code
     9→     ↓
    10→Members Join
    11→     ↓
    12→Share Products
    13→     ↓
    14→Vote / Discuss
    15→     ↓
    16→Add Products to Group Cart
    17→     ↓
    18→Group Checkout
    19→     ↓
    20→One Common Delivery
    21→     ↓
    22→Delivery Cost Shared
    23→```
    24→
    25→Think of it like a **shared shopping room**: everyone can suggest shirts, everyone can vote, but only authorized people can finalize the group's purchase.
    26→
    27→---
    28→

    30→
    31→A logged-in customer can create a group.
    32→
    33→### Create Group
    34→
    35→```text

    37→DIU Friends
    38→

    40→Buying shirts together

    42→Delivery area:
    43→Dhaka

    46→```

    49→
    50→```text
    51→DIU Friends
    52→
    53→Group Code:
    54→BK7X29
    55→
    56→[ Copy Code ]
    57→[ Share Group ]

    59→

    61→
    62→Example:
    63→
    64→```text
    65→BK7X29
    66→```

    70→# 2. Joining a group
    71→
    72→Customer enters:

    99→# 3. 👤 Group roles
   100→
   101→Keep group-specific roles simple.
   102→
   103→### Group Owner
   104→
   105→The person who created the group.
   106→
   107→Can:
   108→
   109→* Change group settings
   110→* Remove members
   111→* Change member permissions
   112→* Manage group cart
   113→* Start group checkout
   114→* Set delivery address
   115→* Close/delete group
   116→
   117→### Group Member
   118→
   119→Can:
   120→
   121→* View group
   122→* Share products
   123→* Vote
   124→* Comment/discuss
   125→* Add products to group cart
   126→* See group cart
   127→* Remove **their own** items
   128→
   129→### Optional: Group Admin
   130→
   131→I'd initially **avoid this**.
   132→
   133→If a group becomes large, you can allow the owner to promote someone to:
   134→
   135→> Group Admin
   136→
   137→But this isn't necessary for V1.
   138→

   141→# 4. 🔐 Group permissions
   142→
   143→I'd use something like:
   144→
   145→| Action                       | Owner | Member |
   146→| ---------------------------- | ----: | -----: |
   147→| View group                   |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   148→| Join                         |     — |      — |
   149→| Invite                       |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   150→| Share product                |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   151→| Vote                         |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   152→| Comment                      |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   153→| Add to group cart            |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   154→| Remove own cart item         |     ✅ |      ✅ |
   155→| Remove another member's item |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   156→| Edit group settings          |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   157→| Remove member                |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   158→| Start checkout               |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   159→| Set common address           |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   160→| Close group                  |     ✅ |      ❌ |
   161→
   162→The key principle:
   163→
   164→> **Members control their own shopping; the owner controls the group.**

   270→# 8. One vote per member
   271→
   272→A member should only have **one active vote per shared product**.
   273→
   274→Example:
   275→
   276→```text
   277→Rahim → 👍
   278→Karim → 👍
   279→Hasan → 👎
   280→```
   281→
   282→Results:
   283→
   284→```text
   285→👍 2
   286→👎 1
   287→```
   288→
   289→If Rahim changes his mind:
   290→
   291→```text
   292→👍 → 👎
   293→```
   294→
   295→The system updates his vote rather than creating another vote.

   302→
   303→Someone votes:
   304→

   306→

   310→
   311→```text

   313→  ≠
   314→Purchase

   319→> **Add to Group Cart**

   503→# 16. 🧮 Delivery splitting
   504→
   505→This is where your idea becomes useful.
   506→
   507→Suppose:
   508→
   509→```text
   510→Rahim     ৳1,398
   511→Karim       ৳699
   512→Hasan       ৳799
   513→----------------
   514→Products  ৳2,896
   515→```
   516→
   517→Normal individual delivery:
   518→
   519→```text
   520→Rahim → ৳80
   521→Karim → ৳80
   522→Hasan → ৳80
   523→
   524→Total delivery = ৳240
   525→```
   526→
   527→Group:
   528→
   529→```text
   530→One destination
   531→       ↓
   532→One delivery
   533→       ↓
   534→৳80
   535→```
   536→
   537→Savings:
   538→
   539→```text
   540→৳240 → ৳80
   541→```
   542→
   543→---
   544→
   545→# 17. How should delivery be split?
   546→
   547→I recommend **proportional splitting based on item subtotal**.
   548→
   549→Example:
   550→
   551→```text
   552→Total product value = ৳2,896
   553→Delivery = ৳80
   554→```
   555→
   556→Rahim:
   557→
   558→```text
   559→1398 / 2896 × 80
   560→≈ ৳38.64
   561→```
   562→
   563→Karim:
   564→
   565→```text
   566→699 / 2896 × 80
   567→≈ ৳19.31
   568→```
   569→
   570→Hasan:
   571→
   572→```text
   573→799 / 2896 × 80
   574→≈ ৳22.05
   575→```
   576→
   577→Total:
   578→
   579→```text
   580→38.64 + 19.31 + 22.05
   581→= ৳80
   582→```
   583→
   584→This is fairer than simply splitting:
   585→
   586→```text
   587→৳80 / 3 = ৳26.67
   588→```
   589→
   590→because members may buy very different amounts.
   591→
   592→---
   593→
   594→# 18. But for COD...
   595→
   596→Here's the important operational problem.
   597→
   598→Suppose:
   599→
   600→```text
   601→Rahim → ৳1,436.64
   602→Karim → ৳718.31
   603→Hasan → ৳821.05
   604→```
   605→
   606→Are you going to ask the delivery agent to collect three separate payments?
   607→
   608→**I wouldn't.**
   609→
   610→For V1:
   611→
   612→### One group order = one COD payment
   613→
   614→The group owner chooses:
   615→
   616→> **COD payer**
   617→
   618→Example:
   619→
   620→```text
   621→COD Payer:
   622→Rahim
   623→
   624→Total:
   625→৳2,976
   626→```
   627→
   628→Rahim collects money from the others himself.
   629→
   630→The system can still show:
   631→
   632→```text
   633→Rahim owes:    ৳1,436.64
   634→Karim owes:      ৳718.31
   635→Hasan owes:      ৳821.05
   636→```
   637→
   638→But Budget King BD only needs to collect:
   639→
   640→```text
   641→৳2,976
   642→```
   643→
   644→from the delivery recipient.
   645→
   646→---
   647→
   648→# 19. Alternative: equal delivery split
   649→
   650→For simplicity, you could allow:
   651→
   652→```text
   653→Delivery splitting:
   654→
   655→○ Proportional
   656→○ Equal
   657→```
   658→
   659→But I would make **proportional the default**.

   814→
   815→If a member leaves:
   816→
   817→```text
   818→Leave Group
   819→```
   820→
   821→their **unsubmitted group-cart items should be removed**.
   822→
   823→But previous completed orders remain unaffected.
   824→
   825→Example:
   826→
   827→```text
   828→Group membership
   829→     ↓
   830→Leave
   831→     ↓
   832→Remove active cart items
   833→     ↓
   834→Preserve order history
   835→```

   840→
   841→Don't allow:

  1201→# 📊 Budget King BD — Inventory System
  1202→
  1203→For Budget King BD, I recommend a **variant-based inventory system with reservations and an immutable movement history**.
  1204→
  1205→The core idea:
  1206→
  1207→```text
  1208→Product
  1209→   ↓
  1210→Variant
  1211→   ↓
  1212→Available Stock
  1213→   ↓
  1214→Reservation
  1215→   ↓
  1216→Order
  1217→   ↓
  1218→Delivered / Cancelled / Returned
  1219→```
  1220→
  1221→The key rule is: **customers never directly modify stock.** Every stock change happens through controlled server-side operations.
  1222→
  1223→---
  1224→
  1225→# 1. 👕 Stock belongs to variants
  1226→
  1227→Inventory should be attached to the **product variant**, not the product itself.
  1228→
  1229→Example:
  1230→
  1231→```text
  1232→Oxford Shirt
  1233→│
  1234→├── Black / M   → SKU: OS-BLK-M → 12
  1235→├── Black / L   → SKU: OS-BLK-L → 8
  1236→├── Black / XL  → SKU: OS-BLK-XL → 5
  1237→├── White / M   → SKU: OS-WHT-M → 10
  1238→└── White / L   → SKU: OS-WHT-L → 7
  1239→```
  1240→
  1241→Each variant has its own stock.
  1242→
  1243→This becomes essential when you eventually sell trousers, shoes, etc.
  1244→
  1245→**Analogy:** A clothing store doesn't have "20 Oxford shirts" as one pile—the warehouse has 20 specific combinations of size/color.
  1246→
  1247→---
  1248→
  1249→# 2. 🗄️ Inventory table
  1250→
  1251→Use:
  1252→
  1253→```text
  1254→inventory
  1255→├── variant_id
  1256→├── quantity
  1257→├── reserved_quantity
  1258→├── low_stock_threshold
  1259→├── updated_at
  1260→└── updated_by
  1261→```
  1262→
  1263→For example:
  1264→
  1265→```text
  1266→Variant: Black / XL
  1267→
  1268→quantity          = 10
  1269→reserved_quantity = 3
  1270→```
  1271→
  1272→Therefore:
  1273→
  1274→```text
  1275→Available stock = 10 - 3 = 7
  1276→```
  1277→
  1278→---
  1279→
  1280→# 3. 🟢 Stock states
  1281→
  1282→You essentially have:
  1283→
  1284→```text
  1285→TOTAL STOCK
  1286→     │
  1287→     ├── AVAILABLE
  1288→     │
  1289→     └── RESERVED
  1290→```
  1291→
  1292→Example:
  1293→
  1294→```text
  1295→Total:      20
  1296→Reserved:    4
  1297→Available:  16
  1298→```
  1299→
  1300→Don't create a separate manually editable `available_quantity`.
  1301→
  1302→Calculate it:
  1303→
  1304→```text
  1305→available = quantity - reserved_quantity
  1306→```
  1307→
  1308→That prevents inconsistencies.
  1309→
  1310→---
  1311→
  1312→# 4. 🛒 Stock reservation
  1313→
  1314→This is particularly important for COD.
  1315→
  1316→Suppose:
  1317→
  1318→```text
  1319→Stock = 10
  1320→```
  1321→
  1322→Customer A orders:
  1323→
  1324→```text
  1325→5 shirts
  1326→```
  1327→
  1328→While the order is pending:
  1329→
  1330→```text
  1331→quantity          = 10
  1332→reserved_quantity = 5
  1333→available         = 5
  1334→```
  1335→
  1336→Now Customer B can't successfully order 6.
  1337→
  1338→The server sees:
  1339→
  1340→```text
  1341→Available = 5
  1342→Requested = 6
  1343→
  1344→❌ Insufficient stock
  1345→```
  1346→
  1347→---
  1348→
  1349→# 5. Why reservation is necessary
  1350→
  1351→Without reservations:
  1352→
  1353→```text
  1354→Stock = 5
  1355→
  1356→Customer A → orders 5
  1357→Customer B → orders 5
  1358→```
  1359→
  1360→Both requests might see:
  1361→
  1362→```text
  1363→Stock = 5
  1364→```
  1365→
  1366→You end up with:
  1367→
  1368→```text
  1369→10 orders
  1370→5 products
  1371→```
  1372→
  1373→That's an **overselling/race-condition problem**.
  1374→
  1375→Reservation acts like putting a product into a physical shopping basket and marking it "held."
  1376→
  1377→---
  1378→
  1379→# 6. When should stock be reserved?
  1380→
  1381→I'd recommend:
  1382→
  1383→```text
  1384→Order successfully created
  1385→        ↓
  1386→Stock reservation
  1387→```
  1388→
  1389→So:
  1390→
  1391→```text
  1392→PENDING
  1393→   ↓
  1394→Reserved
  1395→```
  1396→
  1397→Then the reservation remains while the order is being processed.
  1398→
  1399→---
  1400→
  1401→# 7. Reservation lifecycle
  1402→
  1403→```text
  1404→Order Created
  1405→      ↓
  1406→Reserve Stock
  1407→      ↓
  1408→PENDING
  1409→      │
  1410→      ├── Cancelled → Release Reservation
  1411→      │
  1412→      ↓
  1413→CONFIRMED
  1414→      ↓
  1415→PROCESSING
  1416→      ↓
  1417→SHIPPED
  1418→      ↓
  1419→DELIVERED
  1420→      ↓
  1421→Convert Reservation → Sold
  1422→```
  1423→
  1424→The important distinction:
  1425→
  1426→### Reserved ≠ Sold
  1427→
  1428→A pending order hasn't actually resulted in a completed sale yet.
  1429→
  1430→---
  1431→
  1432→# 8. 📦 When order is shipped
  1433→
  1434→At shipping:
  1435→
  1436→```text
  1437→Reserved
  1438→    ↓
  1439→Sold
  1440→```
  1441→
  1442→Conceptually:
  1443→
  1444→```text
  1445→quantity = quantity - sold_quantity
  1446→reserved_quantity = reserved_quantity - reserved_quantity
  1447→```
  1448→
  1449→Example:
  1450→
  1451→Before:
  1452→
  1453→```text
  1454→Total stock: 10
  1455→Reserved:     3
  1456→Available:    7
  1457→```
  1458→
  1459→After shipping 3:
  1460→
  1461→```text
  1462→Total stock: 7
  1463→Reserved:    0
  1464→Available:   7
  1465→```
  1466→
  1467→---
  1468→
  1469→# 9. ❌ Cancellation
  1470→
  1471→Suppose:
  1472→
  1473→```text
  1474→Stock = 10
  1475→Reserved = 3
  1476→Available = 7
  1477→```
  1478→
  1479→Customer cancels.
  1480→
  1481→The product was never sold.
  1482→
  1483→So:
  1484→
  1485→```text
  1486→Reserved:
  1487→3 → 0
  1488→```
  1489→
  1490→Result:
  1491→
  1492→```text
  1493→Stock = 10
  1494→Reserved = 0
  1495→Available = 10
  1496→```
  1497→
  1498→You **release the reservation**, rather than adding stock.
  1499→

  1502→# 10. 🚚 Delivery failure
  1503→
  1504→If the order was shipped but couldn't be delivered:
  1505→
  1506→```text
  1507→SHIPPED
  1508→   ↓
  1509→DELIVERY_FAILED
  1510→```
  1511→
  1512→You need to distinguish between:
  1513→
  1514→### Reattempt
  1515→
  1516→```text
  1517→DELIVERY_FAILED
  1518→      ↓
  1519→SHIPPED
  1520→```
  1521→
  1522→No inventory change.
  1523→
  1524→### Order permanently cancelled
  1525→
  1526→Then the item comes back into your inventory:
  1527→
  1528→```text
  1529→Cancelled
  1530→   ↓
  1531→Returned to warehouse
  1532→   ↓
  1533→Stock + quantity
  1534→```
  1535→
  1536→But this should happen when the physical goods are actually back—not merely when the order is marked cancelled.
  1537→

  1562→

  1569→Instead record:
  1570→
  1571→```text

  1828→# 20. Reservation expiry
  1829→
  1830→There is one issue:
  1831→
  1832→A customer creates a COD order and then disappears.
  1833→
  1834→If reservations never expire:
  1835→
  1836→```text
  1837→Stock = 10
  1838→Customer reserves 8
  1839→Never responds
  1840→```
  1841→
  1842→Only 2 remain available forever.
  1843→
  1844→So I recommend reservation/order expiration logic.
  1845→
  1846→For example:
  1847→
  1848→```text
  1849→PENDING
  1850→   ↓
  1851→Reservation active
  1852→   ↓
  1853→Customer doesn't confirm
  1854→   ↓
  1855→Reservation expires
  1856→   ↓
  1857→Stock released
  1858→```
  1859→
  1860→The exact timeout should be decided based on your agent workflow.

  1866→Bad:
  1867→
  1868→```text

  1878→
  1879→Good:
  1880→
  1881→```text
  1882→Browser
  1883→  ↓
  1884→Request order
  1885→  ↓
  1886→Server
  1887→  ↓
  1888→Database transaction
  1889→  ↓
  1890→Lock/check inventory
  1891→  ↓
  1892→Reserve stock
  1893→  ↓
  1894→Create order

  1897→This prevents race conditions.
