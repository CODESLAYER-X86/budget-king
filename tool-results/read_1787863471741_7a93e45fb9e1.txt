     1→     1→# 📈 Budget King BD — Analytics & Reporting
     2→     2→
     3→     3→For Budget King BD, analytics should be designed as **operational analytics first**, not a giant BI system. Admin should quickly understand **what is selling, what is happening with orders, who the customers are, and where problems exist**.
     4→     4→
     5→     5→Core structure:
     6→     6→
     7→     7→```text
     8→     8→Orders
     9→     9→ ├── Sales
    10→    10→ ├── Customers
    11→    11→ ├── Products
    12→    12→ ├── Rewards
    13→    13→ ├── Groups
    14→    14→ └── Agents
    15→    15→        ↓
    16→    16→   Analytics Layer
    17→    17→        ↓
    18→    18→ Admin Dashboard / Reports
    19→    19→```
    20→    20→
    21→    21→---

   562→   562→Example:

  1061→  1061→### The key principle
  1062→  1062→

  1064→  1064→

  1066→  1066→
  1067→  1067→```text

  1076→  1076→# ⚙️ Budget King BD — Business Rules
  1077→  1077→
  1078→  1078→This section defines **what the system is allowed to do and under what conditions**. These rules should be agreed upon before implementation because they will affect the database, backend logic, RLS, order workflow, rewards, and UI.
  1079→  1079→
  1080→  1080→---
  1081→  1081→
  1082→  1082→## 1. 💵 COD Rules
  1083→  1083→
  1084→  1084→Since Budget King BD accepts **Cash on Delivery only**:
  1085→  1085→
  1086→  1086→### Basic rules
  1087→  1087→
  1088→  1088→* All customer orders use COD.
  1089→  1089→* No online payment status is required.
  1090→  1090→* Order is not considered financially paid until delivery/payment is confirmed.
  1091→  1091→* Customer sees the total payable amount before confirming.
  1092→  1092→* Delivery charge is included in the final COD amount.
  1093→  1093→* Redeemed vouchers reduce the COD amount.
  1094→  1094→* Coins themselves cannot be used directly as payment unless converted into a voucher.
  1095→  1095→
  1096→  1096→### COD order flow
  1097→  1097→
  1098→  1098→```text
  1099→  1099→Cart
  1100→  1100→ ↓
  1101→  1101→Checkout
  1102→  1102→ ↓
  1103→  1103→COD selected automatically
  1104→  1104→ ↓
  1105→  1105→Order placed
  1106→  1106→ ↓
  1107→  1107→Agent confirms
  1108→  1108→ ↓
  1109→  1109→Order prepared
  1110→  1110→ ↓
  1111→  1111→Shipped
  1112→  1112→ ↓
  1113→  1113→Delivered
  1114→  1114→ ↓
  1115→  1115→COD collected
  1116→  1116→ ↓
  1117→  1117→Order completed
  1118→  1118→```
  1119→  1119→
  1120→  1120→### Important
  1121→  1121→
  1122→  1122→Don't let the customer enter an arbitrary COD amount.
  1123→  1123→
  1124→  1124→```text
  1125→  1125→Product subtotal
  1126→  1126→+ Delivery
  1127→  1127→- Voucher
  1128→  1128→= Final COD amount
  1129→  1129→```
  1130→  1130→
  1131→  1131→The server calculates this.
  1132→  1132→
  1133→  1133→---

  1135→  1135→# 2. ❌ Cancellation Rules
  1136→  1136→
  1137→  1137→You need to distinguish **customer cancellation** from **admin/agent cancellation**.
  1138→  1138→
  1139→  1139→### Customer cancellation
  1140→  1140→
  1141→  1141→Customer can cancel only before a certain stage.
  1142→  1142→
  1143→  1143→Recommended:
  1144→  1144→
  1145→  1145→```text
  1146→  1146→PENDING
  1147→  1147→   ↓
  1148→  1148→CONFIRMED
  1149→  1149→   ↓
  1150→  1150→PROCESSING
  1151→  1151→   ↓
  1152→  1152→SHIPPED
  1153→  1153→   ↓
  1154→  1154→DELIVERED
  1155→  1155→```
  1156→  1156→
  1157→  1157→Allow cancellation:
  1158→  1158→
  1159→  1159→```text
  1160→  1160→PENDING       ✅
  1161→  1161→CONFIRMED     ✅/configurable
  1162→  1162→PROCESSING    ❌
  1163→  1163→SHIPPED       ❌
  1164→  1164→DELIVERED     ❌
  1165→  1165→```
  1166→  1166→
  1167→  1167→Once shipped, the customer should use the return process instead.
  1168→  1168→
  1169→  1169→### Cancellation reasons
  1170→  1170→
  1171→  1171→Use predefined reasons:
  1172→  1172→
  1173→  1173→* Changed my mind
  1174→  1174→* Ordered by mistake
  1175→  1175→* Wrong product
  1176→  1176→* Wrong size/color
  1177→  1177→* Delivery taking too long
  1178→  1178→* Other
  1179→  1179→
  1180→  1180→For "Other":
  1181→  1181→
  1182→  1182→> Require a short explanation.
  1183→  1183→
  1184→  1184→---

  1365→  1365→# 11. 🪙 Rewards during cancellation/return
  1366→  1366→
  1367→  1367→This must connect directly to your rewards ledger.
  1368→  1368→
  1369→  1369→Suppose:
  1370→  1370→
  1371→  1371→```text
  1372→  1372→Order = ৳2,000
  1373→  1373→↓
  1374→  1374→Customer earns 2,000 coins
  1375→  1375→```
  1376→  1376→
  1377→  1377→Then order is cancelled before completion.
  1378→  1378→
  1379→  1379→The system should reverse the earned coins:
  1380→  1380→
  1381→  1381→```text
  1382→  1382→+2,000 coins
  1383→  1383→        ↓
  1384→  1384→Order cancelled
  1385→  1385→        ↓
