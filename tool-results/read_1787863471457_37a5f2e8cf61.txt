     1→     1→# 🪙 Budget King BD — Rewards System
     2→     2→
     3→     3→The rewards system should be designed as a **ledger-based system**, not simply a `coin_balance` field.
     4→     4→
     5→     5→The basic flow:
     6→     6→
     7→     7→```text
     8→     8→Eligible Order
     9→     9→      ↓
    10→    10→Calculate Coins
    11→    11→      ↓
    12→    12→Create Coin Transaction
    13→    13→      ↓
    14→    14→Customer Balance
    15→    15→      ↓
    16→    16→Redeem Coins
    17→    17→      ↓
    18→    18→Receive Voucher
    19→    19→      ↓
    20→    20→Use Voucher on Order
    21→    21→```
    22→    22→
    23→    23→And importantly:
    24→    24→
    25→    25→```text
    26→    26→Cancel / Return
    27→    27→      ↓
    28→    28→Reverse Previously Awarded Coins
    29→    29→```

    60→    60→# 2. What amount qualifies?
    61→    61→
    62→    62→You need to define whether the reward is based on:
    63→    63→
    64→    64→```text
    65→    65→Product subtotal
    66→    66→```
    67→    67→
    68→    68→or:
    69→    69→
    70→    70→```text
    71→    71→Subtotal + delivery
    72→    72→```
    73→    73→
    74→    74→or:
    75→    75→
    76→    76→```text
    77→    77→Final amount after voucher
    78→    78→```
    79→    79→
    80→    80→### I recommend:
    81→    81→
    82→    82→**Eligible purchase amount = merchandise subtotal after discounts, excluding delivery charges.**
    83→    83→
    84→    84→Example:
    85→    85→
    86→    86→```text
    87→    87→Products             ৳2,200
    88→    88→Voucher              -৳100
    89→    89→Delivery                ৳80
    90→    90→---------------------------
    91→    91→Customer pays        ৳2,180
    92→    92→```
    93→    93→
    94→    94→Eligible amount:
    95→    95→
    96→    96→```text
    97→    97→৳2,100
    98→    98→```
    99→    99→
   100→   100→if the voucher applies to merchandise.
   101→   101→
   102→   102→So the customer qualifies for the ৳2,000 reward.
   103→   103→
   104→   104→Delivery should not generate coins.

   109→   109→
   110→   110→**After successful delivery.**

   113→   113→

   116→   116→* Order is placed

   123→   123→  ↓
   124→   124→CONFIRMED
   125→   125→  ↓
   126→   126→SHIPPED

   171→   171→# 5. What happens with ৳4,000?
   172→   172→
   173→   173→This is something you should decide now.
   174→   174→
   175→   175→There are two models.
   176→   176→
   177→   177→### Model A — Threshold reward
   178→   178→
   179→   179→```text
   180→   180→৳2,000+ → 2,000 coins
   181→   181→```
   182→   182→
   183→   183→Whether ৳2,100 or ৳3,900, the customer gets 2,000.
   184→   184→
   185→   185→### Model B — Proportional reward
   186→   186→
   187→   187→```text
   188→   188→৳2,000 → 2,000 coins
   189→   189→৳4,000 → 4,000 coins
   190→   190→৳6,000 → 6,000 coins
   191→   191→```
   192→   192→
   193→   193→I recommend **Model B** if your intention is essentially:
   194→   194→
   195→   195→> 1 taka eligible spending = 1 coin.
   196→   196→
   197→   197→Then the rule becomes much easier to understand.
   198→   198→
   199→   199→But if you specifically want **fixed rewards per threshold**, use Model A.
   200→   200→
   201→   201→Your database should support either.

   314→   314→# 9. Never delete coin transactions
   315→   315→
   316→   316→Suppose:
   317→   317→
   318→   318→```text id="h9m7p4"
   319→   319→+2,000 EARNED
   320→   320→```
   321→   321→
   322→   322→Then the order is returned.
   323→   323→
   324→   324→Don't delete the original transaction.
   325→   325→
   326→   326→Create:
   327→   327→
   328→   328→```text id="q0k5xm"
   329→   329→-2,000 RETURN_REVERSAL
   330→   330→```
   331→   331→
   332→   332→So your history remains:
   333→   333→
   334→   334→```text id="g3l9k1"
   335→   335→+2,000 EARNED
   336→   336→-2,000 RETURN_REVERSAL
   337→   337→----------------------
   338→   338→0
   339→   339→```
   340→   340→
   341→   341→This is much safer for auditing.
   342→   342→
   343→   343→**Analogy:** A bank doesn't erase yesterday's deposit when you withdraw money; it records another transaction.

  1118→  1118→# 35. Final design decisions
  1119→  1119→
  1120→  1120→### 🪙 Coins
  1121→  1121→
  1122→  1122→* Ledger-based
  1123→  1123→* Admin-configurable earning rules
  1124→  1124→* Award after delivery
  1125→  1125→* Delivery fee doesn't earn coins
  1126→  1126→* Support expiration
  1127→  1127→* Earliest-expiring coins consumed first
  1128→  1128→* Never delete transactions
  1129→  1129→* Reversals are new transactions
  1130→  1130→* Negative balances allowed for legitimate reward reversals
  1131→  1131→
  1132→  1132→### 🎟️ Vouchers
  1133→  1133→
  1134→  1134→* Admin-configurable
  1135→  1135→* Fixed or percentage discounts
  1136→  1136→* Configurable coin cost
  1137→  1137→* Optional minimum order
  1138→  1138→* Optional maximum discount
  1139→  1139→* Expiration
  1140→  1140→* One-time use
  1141→  1141→* User-specific
  1142→  1142→* Atomic redemption
  1143→  1143→
  1144→  1144→### 🔄 Returns/cancellations
  1145→  1145→
  1146→  1146→* No reward if order never reaches eligibility
  1147→  1147→* Reverse earned coins after qualifying order is returned
  1148→  1148→* Partial returns recalculate/reverse the excess reward
  1149→  1149→* Unused reward voucher can be cancelled if its qualifying order is fully returned
  1150→  1150→* Already-used vouchers aren't retroactively rewritten; the coin ledger handles the resulting adjustment
  1151→  1151→
  1152→  1152→### 🔐 Security
  1153→  1153→
  1154→  1154→```text
  1155→  1155→Customer
  1156→  1156→   ↓
  1157→  1157→Can READ own rewards
  1158→  1158→   ↓
  1159→  1159→Can REQUEST redemption
  1160→  1160→   ↓
  1161→  1161→Server validates
  1162→  1162→   ↓
  1163→  1163→Atomic transaction
  1164→  1164→   ↓
  1165→  1165→Coin deduction + voucher creation
  1166→  1166→```
  1167→  1167→
  1168→  1168→**The critical rule is that coins and vouchers are financial-like assets even though they aren't real currency. Treat them with the same accounting discipline as money: immutable ledger entries, atomic operations, clear reversals, and complete auditability.**
  1169→  1169→
  1170→  1170→
  1171→  1171→# 🗄️ Budget King BD — Database Design Plan
  1172→  1172→
  1173→  1173→For the first version, I would design the Supabase PostgreSQL database around **10–14 core tables**, with a few supporting tables for variants, groups, rewards, and auditing.
  1174→  1174→
  1175→  1175→The most important principle: **don't store derived business data when it can be calculated safely from authoritative records.** For example, coin balance should come from a transaction ledger rather than being blindly incremented in multiple places.
